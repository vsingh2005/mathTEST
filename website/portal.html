Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kids STEM Studio?

Kids STEM Studio (KSS) provides hands-on interactive programs in coding, game design, robotics, engineering, and math problem solving for students ages 5-14.

How does the diagnostic test place students?

The placement test uses an adaptive algorithm serving 15 targeted questions. Reaching 80% accuracy in a grade domain confirms baseline readiness for that grade level.

How do worksheets unlock?

Taking the placement test immediately unlocks all lessons up to your placed grade. Subsequent lessons unlock consecutively as you score 80% or higher.
